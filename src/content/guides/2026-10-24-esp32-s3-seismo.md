Parts pile on the breadboard — an ESP32-S3 board, a cheap LSM6DSO, and a 2.0″ ST7789 — turned into a **local** station you can follow phase by phase. No cloud, no fleet: COM UART, I2C, SPI, then STA/LTA detection and a Wald 1999 MMI dial on the panel.

Start from a clean Ubuntu box (PlatformIO is not magic), erase the chip, prove hello serial, power TFT then IMU, scan `0x6A`, stream accel, fill the screen, and land on the intensity UI. Each step freezes a `firmware/<nn>-…/` folder so you can replay the path; later extras get new folders, not rewrites.

A breadboard IMU is not a real seismometer — plant it, thump the desk, enjoy the demo, don’t treat the number as ground truth.
