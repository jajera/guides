An ESP32-C3 DevKit, VEML7700, discrete LED, and 240×240 ST7789 become a small lux node that onboards like something you bought: scan the SoftAP QR on the TFT, join Wi‑Fi from your phone, and Home Assistant finds it over the ESPHome API. Mode, schedule, and night lux thresholds live on the device, so Schedule and Night keep working while Wi‑Fi drops. Hold **BOOT** for five seconds to factory-reset prefs only.

The tutorial walks the firmware YAML, builds the ESPHome image on Ubuntu 24.04, wires I2C and SPI on a breadboard (VIN for the VEML, not 3Vo), flashes over USB Serial/JTAG, then covers Mode, Display, and reset checks in Home Assistant.
